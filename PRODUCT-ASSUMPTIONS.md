# CampusBites — Product Assumptions

## 1. What product are you planning to build?

CampusBites is an app for ordering food from your college canteen ahead of time, so you don’t have to wait in the queue.

## 2. What problem does it solve?

Breaks between classes are short, about 10–15 minutes. Students spend most of that standing in the canteen line, and sometimes the food still isn’t ready before the next class starts.

## 3. Who are the target users?

College students who eat at campus canteens, both day scholars and hostel students.

## 4. What is the main use case?

CampusBites helps college students pre-order food from campus canteens, choose a pickup time and collect their food without waiting in long queues.

## 5. What are the core features?

- Browse canteens and food menus.
- Search and filter food items.
- Add, remove and manage cart items.
- Choose pickup time and add order notes.
- Mock login system.
- Track order status.
- View order history.

## 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?

- Users are mainly college students.
- Students have limited break time (10–15 minutes).
- Students want to avoid long queues.
- Users have access to a smartphone and internet.
- Students prefer quick ordering and counter pickup.

## 7. What will be included in your MVP?

- Landing page and canteen listings.
- Food menu with search and filters.
- Food item details.
- Cart and pickup scheduling.
- Order confirmation and tracking.
- Order history.

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

- Online payment.
- Canteen staff dashboard.
- Real-time staff order updates.
- Food delivery services.
